Lexicon Latinum – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

Lexicon Latinum is a Latin–English dictionary, grammar, reader and word-game app. This policy explains what the app does with information, which is: almost nothing.

Data we collect

None. The app has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party software development kits. It does not collect, transmit or sell personal information of any kind.

Data stored on your device

The app keeps your settings (theme, typeface, text size), your saved words, your recent lookups, reading progress and game scores in the app's own local storage on your device. This information never leaves your device and is deleted when you delete the app. In the web version it is kept in your browser's storage for the site and can be cleared from the browser.

Network use

The native iPhone, iPad and Android apps contain the entire dictionary and all content and work fully offline. They make no network requests, except when you choose to open an external link (for example to the source of a text), which opens in your system browser. The web version downloads dictionary data from the site that hosts it as you look words up; no personal data is sent with those requests beyond what any web request includes.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no information from anyone, including children.

Sources and licences

Dictionary text comes from Lewis & Short, A Latin Dictionary (1879), in the Perseus Digital Library edition (CC BY-SA 4.0), and from Whitaker's Words (public domain). Reading passages, paintings and the synonym handbook are public domain.
